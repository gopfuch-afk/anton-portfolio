Ultimate Champions

A fantasy sports experience that brings fans closer to the game. The website connects the excitement of collecting player cards with a clear introduction to playing, managing a team, and competing.

My role
Product Designer
Scope
Website Design
Platform
Web Application

Overview

Ultimate Champions by Unagi lets sports fans collect officially licensed player cards, build their lineups, and compete using their knowledge of the game.

The website introduces this experience to new players, bringing gameplay, collectibles, and club partnerships into one story. The design goal was to turn initial curiosity into an understanding of how to get started.

My role

My focus was on the website experience: connecting the product’s identity with clear content structure and an engaging presentation.

  • Structured the journey from discovering the game to exploring its core mechanics.
  • Connected player cards, gameplay, and partnerships through a shared visual language.
  • Balanced expressive layouts with readable content and clear calls to action.

Challenge

The website needed to explain several connected ideas: fantasy sports, collectible cards, team management, and competition. Presenting everything at once could make the experience feel complicated to newcomers.

The challenge was to capture the energy of sports while giving visitors a straightforward introduction to the game.

Approach

I organized the experience around a progressive introduction: discover the game, understand how to play, explore the cards, and take the next step.

Player imagery and card artwork brought the experience to life, while distinct sections gave each concept room to be understood. Clear calls to action connected product discovery with entry into the game.

Outcome

The resulting design brought gameplay, collectibles, and brand storytelling into a cohesive website experience. It gave visitors a structured path from their first impression to understanding the game’s core appeal.

The contribution was a clearer presentation of a layered product—preserving its visual energy while making the introduction more approachable.
