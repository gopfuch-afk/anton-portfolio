About

I make complex products feel simple.

Hi, I’m Anton

I’m a Senior Product Designer with more than ten years of experience designing web and mobile products for SaaS, AI, Web3, marketplaces and enterprise platforms.

I work closely with founders, product managers and engineers to turn complex workflows into clear, usable interfaces — balancing user needs, business goals and technical feasibility.

I understand front-end development, so the interfaces I design are realistic, scalable and easy to implement. Design systems, Figma variables and tokens help keep products consistent as they grow.

How I work

I start from requirements and constraints, move quickly to interactive prototypes in Figma, and validate ideas before engineering time is spent. Handoff comes with components, variables and tokens that map cleanly to code.
