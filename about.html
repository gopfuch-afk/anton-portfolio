About

I make complex products feel simple.

Hi, I’m Anton

I’m a Senior Product Designer with 9 years of experience designing complex web and mobile products across SaaS, AI, Web3, marketplaces and enterprise platforms.

I help companies turn complex ideas, workflows and data into simple, scalable and intuitive digital products — balancing user needs, business goals and technical feasibility.

I work end-to-end, from early product thinking, research and UX architecture to interaction design, polished UI, design systems and implementation. I collaborate closely with founders, product managers and engineers to make sure the product not only looks good, but works well and can scale.

How I work

I start by understanding the problem — user needs, business goals and technical constraints — before moving into the interface. I simplify complex requirements into clear flows and product logic, then quickly explore and validate ideas through wireframes and interactive prototypes. From there, I design scalable systems using reusable components, patterns and design tokens, while working closely with engineering to make sure every solution is realistic, consistent and ready to build.

How I think about AI

AI should make complex work feel clear.

    Show how the answer was made.

    Keep people in control.

    AI-assisted support in Mava AI
  3. 03

    Prototype the behaviour, not just the screen.
